Privacy Policy

Last updated · 20 September 2026

1. Information we collect

  • Analytics data: FiveStat uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. This may include approximate location, device and browser information, pages visited and time spent on pages.
  • Contact information: If you email FiveStat or voluntarily subscribe to updates, we process the information you provide, including your email address.

2. How we use information

Information is used to operate and improve FiveStat, understand which features are useful, respond to enquiries and send updates where someone has opted in.

FiveStat does not sell personal data. Limited information may be processed by service providers used to operate and measure the site, such as Google Analytics, subject to their applicable privacy terms.

3. Cookies

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for site analytics and to remember preferences such as whether a notice has been dismissed. You can restrict or delete cookies through your browser settings.

4. Your rights

Under UK data-protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict or object to processing, and withdraw consent where consent is the basis for processing.

To exercise these rights, email fivestatanalytics@gmail.com.

5. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as FiveStat develops. The latest version and update date will be published on this page.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to fivestatanalytics@gmail.com.
