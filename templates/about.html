About FiveStat.

Statistical models, predictions and visual analysis across football and motorsport.

What FiveStat builds

01
Football analytics

Premier League match probabilities, correct-score heatmaps, expected-goal analysis, player projections and season simulations.

02
Formula 1 analysis

Driver and constructor ratings, race predictions, championship simulations and fantasy projections.

03
Transparent modelling

Models are tested against historical outcomes and refined when the evidence supports a change. The aim is not to present sport as certain, but to make the probabilities, assumptions and limitations easier to understand.

Behind FiveStat

FiveStat is designed and developed by Jack Maher, combining sports-industry experience with Python modelling, data analysis and web development.

For technical detail, read the methodology and model performance pages.
