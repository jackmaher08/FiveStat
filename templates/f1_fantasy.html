How xFP is calculated

FiveStat estimates unboosted driver fantasy points using its race probabilities and projected grid: qualifying points (10 for pole through 1 for P10), race finishing points (25 for P1 through 1 for P10), changes in race position, expected overtakes (+1 each), fastest lap (+10), Driver of the Day (+10), and an 8% probability of not being classified (-20). On Sprint weekends, Sprint finishing, positions, overtakes, fastest lap and non-classification are also included.

Position, overtake, Driver of the Day and Sprint forecasts use simplified assumptions, not verified future events. Results after qualifying use recorded qualifying positions when available. These estimates do not yet model constructor-specific bonuses, pit-stop scoring, chips, team costs or transfer penalties, so they should not be interpreted as projected total team scores. Season forecasts add manually verified fantasy points for completed rounds to projected points for upcoming rounds. Transfer targets use up to the next five races.
