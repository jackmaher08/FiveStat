How is the Player Watch list built?

Who is included?
Players in all four positions are assessed against a season-aware minutes threshold. Players with sustained recent playing time (at least three 60-minute appearances and a 65-minute average across four or five completed Gameweeks) can qualify earlier. Expected minutes blend recent and season usage where the official FPL match history is available; availability still scales projections down.

Why might a player be missing?
A player may be absent if they have insufficient minutes for the current stage of the season, are unavailable, project below 60 minutes per fixture, have no upcoming fixture data, or lack enough attacking data for a meaningful projection.

How are projections calculated?
Each player's projected xG and xA uses a 70% season / 30% recent blend, applied to FiveStat's team projection and scaled by expected minutes. Points then use position-specific FPL goal, assist and clean-sheet scoring. Historical bonus and defensive contribution estimates are included where data is available; cards and other rare events are not yet modelled.

Which Gameweeks can I select?
Choose the next Gameweek, the next five Gameweeks combined, the rest of the season, or one specific remaining Gameweek through GW38 when full-season projections are available (otherwise the next five Gameweeks). Double Gameweek projections sum both fixtures; a blank Gameweek has zero projected points. Rest of season uses currently scheduled fixtures and current playing-time assumptions, not predicted future injuries or transfers. The 30% recent attacking-form weight is retained for the first five Gameweeks, then halved every five further Gameweeks. Team-level forecasts are unchanged.

What does the Differentials filter show?
The filter limits any selected projection window to players with FPL ownership below 15%.
